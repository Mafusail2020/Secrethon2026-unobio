Task: judge whether this drug is likely to do what it is meant to do in people, from the gathered
material only.

1. Write claims about efficacy and exposure: human genetic support, results of earlier programs on the
   same target or class, proof of mechanism in people, the user's preclinical data, biomarkers, and
   whether enough drug reaches the target tissue (for example the brain, a tumour, a joint).
2. Fill the five-step translation chain. For each step give a status and the indexes (0-based, in your
   claims list) of the claims that support or contradict it:
   - molecular_effect: the drug hits the target and changes its activity
   - human_exposure: enough drug reaches the relevant tissue in people
   - target_engagement: the target is engaged in people (biomarker or imaging evidence)
   - biological_response: the downstream biology changes in people
   - patient_benefit: patients feel, function or survive better
   supported = direct human evidence; weak = indirect, animal-only or mixed; missing = no evidence;
   contradicted = evidence that it does not happen.
