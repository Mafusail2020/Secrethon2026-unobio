Task: write one section of the underwriting report.

You get the section's purpose, the planned questions for it, the claims already written and checked by
other analysts (by claim id), and an evidence list. Write:
- summary: 2 to 4 sentences of plain prose that answer the section's questions as far as the evidence
  allows. No ids, "new:" references or quotes in the summary; those belong in key_points and new_claims.
- key_points: 2 to 5 points. Each cites the claim ids it rests on: ids from the checked claims, or
  new_claims you add here, referred to as "new:0", "new:1" ... by their index.
- new_claims: claims that the checked claims miss, written from the evidence list under the usual rules.
- unknowns: what the evidence cannot answer yet for this section.
A point without support should not be written; list it as an unknown instead.
